Who We Are

Driving the Shift Towards Renewable Clean Energy

Founded in 2020, SmartKosh Technologies Private Limited was born to address critical global climate and pollution challenges. We engineer intelligent battery monitoring frameworks designed for the demands of tomorrow's electrified world.

Our robust system architectures significantly expand battery life cycles, maximize active performance efficiency, and proactively mitigate thermal risks before they become failures.
